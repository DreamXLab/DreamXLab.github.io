<h1>INNOVATION &amp; IMPACTS</h1>

<p><strong>SAM</strong> – Redefine Surgery Intelligence</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>S</strong>upervision – Enable precision surgery through real-time 3D vision and spatial intelligence to improve patient outcomes and operational efficiency.</li>
  <li><strong>A</strong>utomation – Provide autonomous and personalized laparoscope control to alleviate staffing shortages and elevate surgical productivity at scale.</li>
  <li><strong>M</strong>odel – Streamline preoperative planning, intraoperative guidance, and postoperative analysis, while advancing surgeon–AI co-learning with a surgical foundational model.</li>
</ul>
